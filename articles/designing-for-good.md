# 🌱 Designing for Good

All products should have a positive impact on the individual, the community, and the environment.

Product is an outcome of design, and design is an outcome of creative thought. Thought is shaped by intent — so designing for good starts with purity of intent, before a single pixel or line of code exists.

That means shifting focus away from narrow commercial interests toward genuinely positive outcomes. It also means owning the full lifecycle of what we build: not just the product itself, but its byproducts, its second-order effects, and eventually, its disposal.

Good intent isn't a constraint on good business — it's usually the source of it. Products built to genuinely help people tend to earn trust, retention, and advocacy that purely extractive design never can.
